Captured from the India visa-detail page of the Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority on
6 August 2026.

An ordinary Indian travel document requires an entry visa. The diplomatic, official and service
passport exemptions do not apply to this traveller.

For a business or social visit, ICA identifies a signed Form 14A, a recent passport-sized colour
photograph taken within three months, and a copy of the passport biodata page. The passport should
remain valid for at least six months from entry. Original documents and official English
translations are needed where relevant. Form V39A, a Letter of Introduction, and other supporting
documents may be requested case by case.

An application may be submitted online by a Singapore local contact or strategic partner, or via
the nearest Singapore Overseas Mission or its authorised agent. ICA advises applying within 30
days before arrival. The online processing fee is S$30 and is non-refundable. ICA describes a
typical processing time of three working days, excluding submission day, but some cases take
longer. If approved, the submitting local contact, partner or agent can print the e-Visa.
