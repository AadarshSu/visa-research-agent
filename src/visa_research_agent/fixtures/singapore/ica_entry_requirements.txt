OFFLINE FIXTURE — PARAPHRASED OFFICIAL SOURCE SNAPSHOT
Captured from the Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority on 6 August 2026.

Visitors need an individually issued passport with at least six months' validity. Visa-required
short-term travellers must obtain a visa. Short-term visitors should also be able to show onward
travel arrangements and sufficient funds.

Travellers must normally submit the free SG Arrival Card within the three days before arrival,
including arrival day. The SG Arrival Card is not a visa. A yellow-fever vaccination certificate
is required when recent travel or extended transit involved a country with yellow-fever risk.

Admission remains subject to immigration clearance. A valid visa does not guarantee entry, and the
Visit Pass issued at the checkpoint determines the authorised period of stay.
